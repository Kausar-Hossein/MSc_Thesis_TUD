08 / Workshop participation

Learning together.
Connecting ideas to practice.

Practical learning and collaborative discussion at the intersection of geospatial science, climate adaptation and early warning.

01
Geospatial methods

QGIS: From Spatial Data to Dynamic Visualisation

Working with point clouds and mesh datasets, I explored how QGIS can turn complex spatial information into interpretable visualisations. Hands-on exercises used hourly precipitation data to animate changes across an area over the course of a day, alongside visualisation of wind patterns. The workshop connected spatial data processing with time-enabled mapping, making environmental patterns easier to explore and communicate.

QGIS · Point clouds · Mesh datasets · Temporal visualisation
02
Climate & society

Climate Decisions: Agriculture, Communities & Policy

Through role-play, debate and group discussion, I explored how climate change affects agriculture, livelihoods and socioeconomic development. Participants considered the perspectives of farmers, communities and public authorities, discussing how people respond to climate risks and how policy choices shape local and regional outcomes. The exercise brought the trade-offs of climate adaptation into focus, showing how decisions made at one level can influence people and resources at another.

Climate adaptation · Role-play · Agriculture · Decision-making
03
AI & early warning

AI for Flood Forecasting & Community Preparedness

This workshop explored how artificial intelligence and machine learning can support flood forecasting and the assessment of climate-related extreme events. Discussions connected predictive modelling with practical early warning: how to communicate timely information so communities can prepare, protect livelihoods and reduce potential losses. I examined the role of data-driven tools in linking scientific forecasts with decisions before an event unfolds.

Artificial intelligence · Flood forecasting · Early warning · Preparedness
